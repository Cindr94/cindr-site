About Cindr Group

A Wyoming-Registered LLC, Built for Accountability

Cindr Group LLC is a Wyoming-registered limited liability company serving as a responsive, accountable prime and broker for government procurement. We hold the contract, we own the outcome, and we coordinate a vetted network of suppliers and manufacturers to deliver — so your agency works with one point of contact instead of many.

Cindr Group is currently focused on federal contracting opportunities. The same accountable, single-partner model extends to state, local, and commercial buyers as those relationships develop.
